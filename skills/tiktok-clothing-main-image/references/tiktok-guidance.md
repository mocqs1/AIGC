# TikTok Guidance

This reference records product decisions from
`docs/product/tiktok-clothing-main-image-research.md`. It is guidance, not a
platform approval guarantee; re-check the target market's Seller Center and ad
specification before publishing.

## Purpose and framing

| Purpose | Default composition | Detail priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `shop_listing` | Complete product, vertical 9:16, neutral studio | Color, silhouette, product identity |
| `photo_carousel` | Master first, then same-product wear/detail views | One verifiable attribute per frame |
| `video_cover` | Cover-safe 9:16, product centered away from UI edges | Recognizable product at thumbnail size |
| `ugc_variant` | Adult creator-style, natural light, product still dominant | Demonstration without fake testimonial |

Supported styles:

- `studio_detail`: calibrated background, controlled light, macro-capable
  framing, and no distracting props.
- `creator_ugc`: handheld or lived-in context with restrained copy; it must not
  imply a real customer's review or outcome.

## Copy and safety

Only render factual attributes supplied by the user or catalog data. Keep text
short and legible. Do not add prices, discounts, QR codes, URLs, platform logos,
"Best Seller", "Trending", fake reviews, before/after body changes, medical or
weight-loss claims, or unsupported performance promises.

For underwear and sleepwear, use adults, complete coverage, ordinary clothing
poses, and non-sexualized camera angles. Ambiguous age, transparency, coverage,
body alteration, or fine-detail fidelity always remains `manual_review`.

## Quality gate

Before publishing, compare the master and output at 1:1 and thumbnail scale:

1. Product color, pattern, logo, silhouette, and proportions match.
2. Fabric texture, seams, edges, straps, closures, and labels remain readable.
3. No garment area is cropped or hidden by UI-safe-zone placement, props, blur,
   or dramatic shadow.
4. Any overlay text is factually sourced and does not make a prohibited claim.
5. The output's policy status is reviewed for the target market and account.
